Draft flow map

Three screens, 19 states. Setup leads to the room, the room leads to the report. All content is invented. The draft is static: nothing is saved or sent.

Setup (5 states)

Choose a room, bank, style and length. Past sessions below.

  • DefaultLast-used choices, two past sessions.
  • EmptyNo banks found, no sessions, start disabled.
  • LoadingBanks loading, session rows pending.
  • ErrorServer unreachable, retry.
  • Long contentLong role context scrolls inside its box.

Room (9 states)

Dark by default. Add ?bg=light or ?bg=dark to check captions over both backgrounds.

  • DefaultFirst question shown, waiting for the candidate.
  • Interviewer speakingMarker active, captions follow the voice.
  • Candidate speakingLive words, mic meter high.
  • Silence promptInterviewer asks if you want the question repeated.
  • Room loadingProgress with a skip room button.
  • Room unavailablePlain backdrop with a notice.
  • Mic deniedBlocked microphone, retry.
  • Provider errorVoice failed, retry.
  • EndingSaving, then open the report.

Report (5 states)

Written feedback after the session. No prediction of a hiring outcome.

  • DefaultSummary, delivery, two questions, drill.
  • GeneratingStage label while the report is written.
  • ErrorTranscript readable, retry button.
  • EmptyNo answers, nothing to review.
  • Long contentLong answers collapse with show more.
